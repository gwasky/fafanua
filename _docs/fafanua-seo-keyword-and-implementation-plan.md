# Fafanua Website — SEO Keyword & Implementation Plan

## Objective

Improve Fafanua Technologies' organic search visibility for organisations looking for data and analytics services in Uganda and East Africa.

The SEO strategy must balance two things:

1. **Discovery language** — the conventional service terms prospective clients actually search for.
2. **Fafanua positioning** — the distinct promise of helping organisations build and strengthen trusted data foundations.

SEO should improve relevance, crawlability, authority, internal linking and search-engine understanding without turning the site into keyword-heavy marketing copy.

---

# 1. Protected Brand Language

The following phrases are protected Fafanua positioning language:

> **trusted data foundation**

and

> **build a data foundation you can trust**

The homepage must continue to visibly include:

> **Build a data foundation you can trust.**

Do not remove, rewrite, dilute or replace this phrase during SEO optimisation.

Use **trusted data foundation** naturally on relevant service and solution pages where it strengthens the positioning.

## Important distinction

These phrases are the **brand proposition**, not necessarily the highest-volume acquisition keywords.

Search acquisition should primarily use buyer terminology such as:

- data engineering;
- data analytics;
- business intelligence;
- data warehousing;
- data integration;
- data governance;
- data quality;
- managed data services.

The intended model is:

> **Search with familiar service terminology → discover Fafanua → understand the trusted-data-foundation proposition.**

Do not force the protected phrases into every title, heading or metadata field.
